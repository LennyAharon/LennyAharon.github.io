Lenny Aharon

PhD Student, Columbia University

I'm an AI researcher and PhD student in Statistics at Columbia University, working with Liam Paninski at the Zuckerman Institute. I take AI from paper to production: 3D computer vision and foundation models for neuroscience, continual learning and parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and mechanistic interpretability of LLMs. My work includes a NeurIPS 2026 Oral, CVPR and AAAI workshop oral and spotlight talks, and Lightning Pose 3D, an open-source framework adopted by labs worldwide. I also earned my BA and MA at Columbia.

Alongside my PhD I was an AI Researcher at Goldman Sachs, building LLM systems for entity resolution at billion-record scale, led applied AI at Nanose Medical on breath-based disease diagnostics, and worked on frontier-model evaluation with the GenAI team at Scale AI.

Before academia I played professional soccer in Israel, including for the Israel national youth teams, before joining the team at UNC Chapel Hill on a full scholarship.
